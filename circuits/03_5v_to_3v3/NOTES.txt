03 — 5 V → 3.3 V REGULATOR (MCU RAIL)
=====================================

WHY THIS EXISTS
---------------
ESP32-C3 with Wi-Fi needs a solid **≥500 mA** 3.3 V rail.

Take **SYS_5V** from the **TPS61023** boost (circuit 02) — not from
bq25185 `V+`/`VSYS` (that rail is only 3.0–4.5 V and collapses near
empty battery, so a 3.3 V LDO from V+ will brown out).

FLOW
----
    SYS_5V (+5 V from TPS61023)
         ──► 5→3.3 regulator ──► VCC_3V3 ──► ESP32, microSD, OLED, logic

NECESSARY PIECES
----------------
1. Regulator IC or your proven 5→3.3 module/circuit.
2. Input ceramic near VIN (e.g. 10 µF).
3. Output ceramic + bulk near VOUT (e.g. 10 µF + 100 nF).
4. Extra 100 nF at ESP32 VDD pins (circuit 05).

CANDIDATE PARTS (pick one family and lock PN)
---------------------------------------------
LDO (simple, some heat at high Iq):
  - AP2112K-3.3 (600 mA) — common, SOT-23-5
  - TLV1117LV-33 / AMS1117-3.3 (check dropout + heat from 5V)

Buck (cooler, more efficient for Wi-Fi peaks):
  - Small 5→3.3 buck module you already trust
  - Or IC e.g. TPS62840 / similar 1 A class (verify stock/footprint)

If you already have a working 5→3.3 daughterboard from earlier work:
  reuse that schematic/footprint here.

KICAD
-----
Nets: SYS_5V → VIN; VCC_3V3 → ESP32 / SD / UI.
Keep switcher inductors away from antenna keep-out if using a buck.
Thermal relief / copper for LDO if used.

TEST
----
SYS_5V ≈ 5 V → VCC_3V3 ≈ 3.3 V.
Enable Wi-Fi + SD read: rail stays ≥ 3.2 V (scope/multimeter).
No reset loops under TX peaks.
