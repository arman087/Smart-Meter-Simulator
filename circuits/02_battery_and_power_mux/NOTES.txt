02 — SEEED LIPO RIDER PLUS (DAUGHTERBOARD)
=========================================

CHOSEN SOLUTION
---------------
Seeed Lipo Rider Plus — charger + 5V boost.
Use as a **daughterboard** on headers / pads on the main PCB.

    Wiki: https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Lipo-Rider-Plus/
    SKU:  106990290

WHAT WE USE FROM IT
-------------------
- USB-C → charge 1S LiPo (fast charge up to 5V/2A)
- JST → battery
- Header **5V**  → SYS_5V on main PCB (up to 5V/2.4A available)
- GND
- EN (optional) → GPIO or leave open so 5V stays on
- USB-A → ignore

WHAT WE DO NOT USE
------------------
- Header **3V3** — only **250 mA**. Too weak for ESP32-C3 with Wi-Fi.
  Main PCB makes its own 5V→3.3V (circuit 03), ≥500 mA.

POWER FLOW
----------
    Lipo Rider 5V ──► SYS_5V
                         ├─► circuit 03 → VCC_3V3 → ESP32 / SD / OLED
                         └─► circuit 04 TPS2662 → P1_5V → RJ12 pin1

HEADER (J3) ORDER (Seeed)
-------------------------
1 USB | 2 GND | 3 BAT | 4 GND | 5 5V | 6 GND | 7 EN | 8 3V3

Main PCB: bring out at least GND, 5V, EN. Leave 3V3 unconnected (or TP only).

MAIN PCB
--------
- Pads / header matching Lipo Rider (your footprint)
- Board USB-C for ESP32 D+/D− only (circuit 01)
- No on-board LiPo charger (inside Rider)
- P1 foldback is circuit 04, not on the Rider

MECHANICALS
-----------
  ../../mechanicals/lipo_rider_plus/

ALT MODULE
----------
Adafruit #6106 bq25185 + 5V boost — same role (5V out); still need circuit 03.

TEST
----
Battery only: SYS_5V present; VCC_3V3 from circuit 03 (not Rider 3V3).
USB-C on Rider: charges; rails stay up.
Unplug Rider USB-C: still running from LiPo.
Ghost on RJ12 gets 5V via TPS2662.
