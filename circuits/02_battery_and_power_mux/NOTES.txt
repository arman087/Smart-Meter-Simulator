02 — POWER: bq25185 + TPS61023 (INTEGRATE ON MAIN PCB)
======================================================

CHOSEN SOLUTION
---------------
Copy Adafruit product #6106 onto the main PCB (open-source Eagle/KiCad):

  https://www.adafruit.com/product/6106
  Ref files: ../../my_design/libraries/adafruit_bq25185_5v_boost/

Two ICs — **keep both**:

  1) bq25185  — USB/DC charge, power path, battery BUVLO, VSYS out
  2) TPS61023 — boost VSYS → fixed +5 V (SYS_5V)

Do **not** omit the boost. Do **not** use Lipo Rider Plus as the primary power design.

NETS (IMPORTANT)
----------------
  VBUS / VIN   USB-C (and optional DC pads — omit solar if unused)
  VBAT         1S LiPo (2-wire OK; IC has charge terminate + BUVLO)
  VSYS / V+    bq25185 pin 1 — system rail
                 • ~4.5 V with USB
                 • ~3.0–4.2 V on battery only
                 • THIS IS NOT 5 V
  SYS_5V       TPS61023 output — regulated +5 V → rest of board
  EN           boost enable (Adafruit: pull-up to V+; can GPIO later)

POWER FLOW
----------
    USB-C ──► bq25185 ──► V+ / VSYS ──► TPS61023 ──► SYS_5V
                   │                         │
                LiPo                    ┌────┴────┐
                                        ▼         ▼
                                   circuit 03  circuit 04
                                   VCC_3V3     TPS2662 → P1_5V

WHY KEEP TPS61023
-----------------
- RJ12 / Ghost need ~5 V on battery (V+ alone is often 3.3–4.2 V).
- 5→3.3 LDO needs headroom; from a near-empty cell V+ cannot make 3.3 V.

WHAT TO COPY / DROP FROM #6106
------------------------------
Copy: USB-C charge, JST/battery, bq25185, TPS61023, input diodes, ISET/VSET,
      boost inductor + FB resistors, status LEDs if you want.
Drop: solar/DC terminal if unused; green 5V terminal block (use nets instead).
Layout: keep Adafruit inductor/charger area close to their reference.

MAIN PCB ALSO HAS
-----------------
- Separate (or carefully shared) USB-C for ESP32 D+/D− (circuit 01)
- 5→3.3 (circuit 03), TPS2662 (circuit 04), etc.

TEST
----
USB charge: CHG LED; VBAT rises to ~4.2 V.
Battery only: SYS_5V still ≈ 5 V; V+ tracks cell.
Fault / empty: boost or load drops cleanly (BUVLO) — no silent deep discharge mess.
Ghost on RJ12 gets ~5 V via TPS2662.
