04 — P1 +5 V WITH FOLDBACK (DSMR-ALIGNED)
=========================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
RJ12 pin 1 must SOURCE +5 V like a Dutch P1 meter:

  Continuous     IL_CONT ≈ 250 mA
  Overload trip  ≈ 260–300 mA window (DSMR)
  Foldback       on sustained fault / short → ISC ≤ 50 mA
  Then recover when the fault is gone

Official rules: Netbeheer Nederland P1 Companion Standard v5.0.2 §5.3
(power supply + foldback). Signal side (Request / Data) uses optocouplers
and open-collector Data — that is separate (circuits 07 / 08).

Do **not** cite random GitHub “simulators”. Use the standard + TI’s
P1 protection design.


THE PART THAT MATCHES THIS
--------------------------
**TI TPS2662** eFuse (adjustable ~25 mA … 880 mA)

TI wrote this **for smart-meter P1 +5 V**:

  App note (PDF): https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/slvaf94
  Title: Fold Back Current Limiting on User Ports of Smart Meters
         (TPS2662; ILIM near DSMR continuous; foldback ~tens of mA on fault).

  Product: https://www.ti.com/product/TPS2662

Buy DigiKey / Mouser: search **TPS2662**.
Optional: TPS2662 EVM to learn, then copy onto the simulator PCB.


HOW IT FITS YOUR BOARD
----------------------
  Lipo Rider 5V (SYS_5V)   ← circuit 02; Rider 3V3 unused
       │
       ├─► 5→3.3 (circuit 03) → MCU
       │
       ▼
  TPS2662 eFuse  (ILIM ≈ 250–300 mA, foldback path per slvaf94)
       │
       ▼
  P1_5V ──► (± isolated DC-DC, circuit 11) ──► RJ12 pin 1
  (+ bulk ceramic near the jack for OSM inrush)

TI’s note also shows a brief “boost ILIM at startup” trick with an MCU
GPIO so Ghost can charge its caps without sitting forever in foldback.
Optional but smart if Ghost has large bulk caps.


OTHER OPTIONS (WEAKER MATCH)
----------------------------
- Generic 5 V eFuse (TPS2595, STEF05): current **limit**, often not true
  DSMR foldback to ≤50 mA. OK for crude lab, not “as in the standard”.
- Classic LM317 foldback resistor network: educational, not preferred.


KICAD
-----
Place TPS2662 on SYS_5V → P1_5V. Follow slvaf94 schematic (RILIM, soft-start,
FLT, optional MCU ILIM boost). Thick P1_5V / GND to RJ12.


TEST
----
Open: P1_5V ≈ 5 V.
~200 mA load: stays ≥ ~4.9 V.
Hard short pin1–pin6: current collapses into foldback (~tens of mA),
not stuck at 250 mA heating forever; recovers when short removed.
